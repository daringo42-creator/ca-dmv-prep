Two clocks, two different deadlines

You have 10 days for the license and 20 for the vehicles

California Vehicle Code §12505 lets you drive on a Texas license for no more than 10 days after you establish residency. Registration is a separate, longer clock. Set the date below and this page will do the counting.

Residency is triggered by things like registering to vote, paying in-state tuition, claiming a homeowner’s property tax exemption, or taking a job here — not simply by arriving.
